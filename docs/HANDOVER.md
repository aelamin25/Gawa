## The three problems, in plain words

1. **Faces change when nothing changed.** Wake shows the wide-eyed face, a miss shows the thoughtful face, a second miss shows the lotus face, every reply pulls a random drawing from the family. The penguin looks moody. Rule for next time: **the face only changes when the feeling changes.** Listening and misses keep the current face and change only the text and the "(((" mark. One drawing per family per conversation (variety across days, not within a minute).
2. **It feels empty.** The loop is wake, question, reply, miss, miss, close. The kind lines are fine; the shape is not. The user never gets the one moment that is the whole point: *I talked, it heard me.* We need a clear end of every conversation where Pengu shows it heard you (feeling face if caught, otherwise a warm "I HEARD YOU" with a heart), and gaps like "I am sad", "help me", "having fun" filled.
3. **Black flashes.** The full (flashing) refresh runs when Pengu goes back to resting after ten quick updates. Move it to the moment it falls asleep (ten quiet minutes) and to boot only. Nobody is watching then.

Also asked for: **a timeout, and the gear button starts a new conversation.** Short press of the gear = "Hey Pengu" (wake, listening, fresh chat, clears any old state). Every conversation has a hard end (the 10 s quiet already exists; make sure the window cap also ends it cleanly, with the "heard you" moment, never silence).

## Next chat: work on the conversational loop

Build v11 around one simple loop and nothing else:

1. **Start**: "Hey Pengu" or gear button. Face stays as it was, "(((" mark appears, text "I AM LISTENING".
2. **Talk** for up to 10 s (quiet for 3 s ends it early). While you talk the mark pulses; the face does not change.
3. **Heard**: the moment it ends, one of two things, always:
   - feeling caught: that family's face and one kind line (face changes now, and only now);
   - nothing caught: same face, "I HEARD YOU" plus a small heart. Never "say that again", never a shrug.
4. **Stay**: the face and line stay two minutes, then the idle face. Follow-up talk keeps the chat open as today but without face churn.
5. **End**: "thank you" / "good night" / gear long press, or quiet. Full refresh only at sleep time.

Things to add while in there, cheap ones only: "I am sad / I am happy / I am tired / I am angry" plain forms; "help me" ("I AM HERE. GO ON"); "having fun". Keep the 400 limit: drop the least likely chunks first (dups.cpp and count.cpp tell you the numbers).

Things to leave alone: the cloud (parked in `extras/cloud-brain`), new faces (99 is plenty), new families beyond the gaps above.
