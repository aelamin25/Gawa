## Conversation test (v8 and later)

1. Say "Hey Pengu". Ears up.
2. Say "I passed my driving test". Proud face. A small "(((" mark top left means it is still listening.
3. Without saying Hey Pengu, say "and I got the job". Happy face. Still listening.
4. Say "thank you". Grateful face, the "(((" mark goes away. Conversation over.
   (To stop without thanking: "okay stop", "that is all" or "bye pengu". A bare "stop" no longer counts.)
5. Say "I am sad" with no Hey Pengu. Nothing should happen. That is correct.
6. Say "hey pengu how are you" in one breath. It should answer straight away.

Pass = all six behave as written.

## Day question test (v9)

1. Say "Hey Pengu, how are you". It should answer and ask "GOOD OR BAD?".
2. Say "pretty good". Happy face, "YAY! GLAD IT WAS GOOD". (Single words like "good" no longer count: say "it was good" or "pretty good".)
3. Say "Hey Pengu, how are you", then mumble nonsense. Same face, "MM HMM. GO ON". No black flash.
4. Say "not great". Sad face, "OH NO. BIG HUG".

## Leave-it-alone test (v9)

1. Say "Hey Pengu" and then say nothing. After 10 seconds: "I AM HERE WHEN YOU NEED ME".
2. Say "Hey Pengu" with the TV or music on, and say nothing. It must still give up after 10 seconds, not listen forever.
3. Say "Hey Pengu, how are you", answer "it was good", then say nothing for 10 seconds. The happy face stays, the "(((" mark goes.
4. Say "good night pengu". Sleepy face straight away, no two minute wait.
